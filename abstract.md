We formalize the complexity class $\mathrm{P}$ and prove that it is closed under
complement. We prove that the stack-machine and elementary single-tape
definitions coincide, with polynomial bounds covering input conversion and
output cleanup. Requiring finite work-stack alphabets leaves the class unchanged.
